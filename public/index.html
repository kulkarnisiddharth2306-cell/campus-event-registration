The Physics of Gravitational Acceleration

Gravity is the fundamental attractive force between masses. On any celestial body, the weight of an object is directly proportional to its mass and the local gravitational acceleration ($g$):

Fundamental Formula: Weight (N) = Mass (kg) × Gravitational Acceleration (m/s²)

Potential Energy: Eₚ = m × g × h (Joules)
